Tips for breaking into FinTech as a New Grad

SC
Sudiksha Chaurasia Alumni

Senior Software Engineer @ Google • 2 hours ago

I've seen a lot of juniors asking about the specific skill sets needed for FinTech. Beyond just coding, you need a solid grasp of distributed systems and a basic understanding of financial markets.

Here are my top three tips for those looking to land their first role in the industry:

  • Master SQL: Financial data is relational by nature. You need to be able to write complex joins and understand indexing for high-performance queries.
  • Understand ACID properties: Transactional integrity is non-negotiable in finance. Know why it matters and how databases guarantee it.
  • Build a project using a financial API: Use Plaid or Alpaca to build something real. Show that you can handle real-world financial data streams.

Good luck out there! It's a challenging but incredibly rewarding field to work in.
